Our order promise

Every box from Fateema’s Bites is prepared especially for your order. To protect the freshness and quality of our snacks, payments are final once an order has been placed and we are unable to offer refunds after payment. Please check your basket, delivery details, selected date, and any dietary notes carefully before completing checkout. We begin organising ingredients and reserving a production slot as soon as your order is confirmed. If you need to make an adjustment, contact us as quickly as possible. Orders may be cancelled only within 30 minutes of being placed; after that time, preparation may have started and cancellations cannot be accepted. These terms help our small kitchen avoid waste while ensuring every customer receives a freshly made, carefully packed treat. Thank you for understanding and for supporting a small business made with love.
